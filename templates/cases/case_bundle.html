Plaint Under Order VII Rule 1 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908

The Plaintiff above-named respectfully submits that this suit is instituted for recovery of agreed consideration under contract, permanent injunction restraining invocation of bank guarantees, and consequential reliefs arising out of execution of infrastructure works.

1. The Plaintiff is a registered legal entity having its principal office at New Delhi and carrying on business in accordance with law.

2. On or about March 14th, the Defendant issued a letter of intent followed by formal contract execution. The contract specified explicit payment milestones subject to verification of progress certificates.

3. Despite timely submission of milestone deliverables, the Defendant arbitrarily withheld payments totaling the principal claim sum without lawful justification or prior contractual notice.

4. Cause of Action: The cause of action arose on dates specified in paragraph 12 when defendant refused disbursement upon formal notice of dispute.

5. Valuation & Reliefs Prayed: The Plaintiff prays for a decree for the sum claimed together with pendente-lite interest at 12% per annum until realization.

Advocate for Plaintiff: Learned Counsel
Verified on Oath: Authorized Signatory
Written Statement & Counter-Claim of Defendant

The Defendant above-named respectfully submits this Written Statement in response to the Plaint, denying each and every allegation save and except what is specifically admitted herein.

Preliminary Objections: The suit is barred by limitation and does not disclose a valid cause of action against the answering defendant. The plaintiff has willfully suppressed material default notices.

Merits: With reference to paragraphs 1 to 5, the defendant states that the deliverables submitted failed independent inspection tests. Under clause 14.2 of the agreement, liquidated damages were rightly adjusted against running accounts.

Counter-Claim: The defendant claims damages for project delay and costs occasioned by remedial works undertaken through third-party contractors.

Joint Statement of Issues Proposed for Determination

Pursuant to Order X, Rule 1 of the Code of Civil Procedure and the Practice Directions for Case Management Hearings, the parties jointly submit the following issues for consideration by the Bench:

  1. Whether the suit has been validly instituted by an authorized person on behalf of the plaintiff? (OPP)
  2. Whether the plaintiff fulfilled contractual milestones in accordance with quality specifications? (OPP)
  3. Whether the defendant was contractually justified in levying liquidated damages under clause 14.2? (OPD)
  4. Whether the defendant is entitled to recovery under its counter-claim? (OPD)
  5. What reliefs, if any, are the parties entitled to?
